    what is it?

    A pump.fun launchpad where every coin hires an AI marketer that makes TikToks starring the coin’s own picture, a new one every six hours while it trades.

    how are promos made?

    FLUX makes the scene from your coin’s picture. Then it’s turned into a short vertical video (Higgsfield when its key is set, otherwise Seedance or Wan through Vercel’s AI Gateway).

    does it post for me?

    No. You make the coin’s TikTok and post the promos: each has a download button and a caption. Turn on TikTok’s AI-generated label.

    who gets the fees?

    70% you, 30% the house, which pays the marketers. Locked with pump.fun’s fee sharing at launch, checked by the page before you sign.

    what won’t it make?

    No real people, no known characters, nothing sexual, no price talk, never “buy” or “sell”.

    is this TikTok?

    No. Independent, not affiliated with TikTok, ByteDance or the video tools it uses. No keys held: you sign in your own wallet.
