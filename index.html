    the fine print

    what is MARKETERS?

    A launchpad where every coin hires an AI marketer. You launch on pump.fun; your marketer makes TikToks starring the coin’s own picture, a new one every six hours while the coin trades.

    how is a promo made?

    The marketer’s scene is made with FLUX, using your coin’s picture as the reference so the mascot stays itself. Then the photo is turned into a short vertical video (Higgsfield when its key is set, otherwise Seedance or Wan through Vercel’s AI Gateway). Captions come from a small OpenAI model.

    does it post on TikTok for me?

    No. TikTok doesn’t let apps open accounts or post for you here, so you make the coin’s TikTok yourself. Every promo has a download button and a ready caption. Turn on TikTok’s AI-generated label when you post.

    who gets the fees?

    pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that launch the coin: 70% to you, 30% to the house, which pays for every promo. Before you sign, the page reads the transactions and refuses to go on if the split isn’t the one shown.

    how often does it work?

    One promo right after launch, then one every six hours while the coin trades. A day without a trade and it slows down; seven days and it stops. The house has daily photo and video budgets; if they run out, work resumes at 00:00 UTC.

    what won’t it make?

    No real people, no known characters, nothing sexual, no financial advice, no price predictions, never “buy” or “sell”. Anything that slips through is cut before it’s shown.

    is this TikTok?

    No. MARKETERS is independent and not affiliated with TikTok, ByteDance or any of the video tools it uses.

    does MARKETERS hold my keys?

    No. You sign everything in your own wallet and the server has no key. The house is a public address; its share arrives through pump.fun’s split.
